Scope

This notice covers the free, ad-free Android store version of Momo Lernreise from 0.24.0 and these information pages. There are no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, user accounts, chat or live AI services. If those features change, this notice will be updated before introduction.

Local progress and drawings

Learning group, language, settings, flowers and solved tasks are stored only on the device. The app does not ask for a child’s name, email or date of birth. Finger drawings stay in memory and are not uploaded. Progress is not sent to us; Android app backup is disabled. Storage supports the requested settings and continued progress (section 25(2)(2) TDDDG). Where personal data is involved, it serves provision of requested app functions (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Deletion and retention

Local progress remains until reset or app removal. For adults → Reset local progress deletes learning records; basic settings may remain. Android’s clear app data setting or uninstalling removes all local app data. We cannot access or delete these records remotely. There are no additional user accounts to delete.

System speech

Preset German, Turkish and English game text is passed to the device’s speech service. The child’s voice is not recorded and their name is not passed. No microphone, camera or location permission is required. Depending on the provider and voice, the service may use internet and process technical data under its own terms. Choose an installed offline voice for offline speech, or turn sound off. Text and pictures remain available without a matching voice.

Support and contact

If you contact us by email or telephone, we process the contact details and content you provide to handle your request. Please do not send sensitive information or recordings of your child. Contacting us is not required to play. The basis is our legitimate interest in responding to requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or Article 6(1)(b) for contractual matters. Requests are deleted after final resolution unless legal retention duties or outstanding legal claims require retention.

These services are separate from local gameplay. We do not sell data or embed advertising or analytics services in the app.
